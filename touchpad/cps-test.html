What this actually tests

This measures raw click throughput over a fixed window — how many "click" events your browser receives divided by the seconds elapsed. It's a decent proxy for mouse responsiveness and clicking technique (jitter-clicking vs. butterfly-clicking vs. normal), but it can't distinguish a fast human from software that automates clicks — a suspiciously high, perfectly even score is usually the tell.

Getting a more accurate score

  1. Use your normal grip and clicking technique — jitter-clicking will score much higher than a standard click.
  2. Run it 3-4 times; the first attempt is often lower while you find your rhythm.
  3. A wired mouse and a browser without heavy background tabs open will read slightly more accurately than a laggy setup.
