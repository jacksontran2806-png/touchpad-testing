Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Hardware Test Hub ("this site", "we") handles information when you visit hardwaretesthub.net.

Information we collect

The testing tools themselves run entirely in your browser. Pointer positions, clicks, scroll events, keypresses, and gesture data used by the tests are processed locally on your device and are never sent to any server or stored by us.

Like most websites, our hosting and analytics providers may automatically log standard technical information, such as your approximate location (derived from IP address), browser type, device type, and pages visited, for security and basic usage statistics.

Cookies and third-party advertising

This site may display advertisements served by Google AdSense. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies (including the DoubleClick cookie) to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site and other sites on the internet. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this site and/or other sites.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

Your cookie choices

On your first visit, a banner asks whether you accept cookies for advertising and measurement. Until you accept, this site runs with Google Consent Mode set to denied for ad storage, ad personalization, ad user data, and analytics storage — so ads shown before a choice is made are non-personalized.

Your choice is remembered in your browser's local storage under the key hth-cookie-consent. It stays on your device and is never transmitted to us. To change or withdraw your choice, clear this site's data in your browser settings; the banner will appear again on your next visit.

Your browser's own cookie controls remain available regardless, and blocking cookies entirely does not stop the testing tools from working — they need no cookies at all.

Analytics

We may use a privacy-respecting analytics tool to understand aggregate traffic patterns (e.g. which pages are popular). This data is anonymized or aggregated and is not used to identify individual visitors.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Related policies
